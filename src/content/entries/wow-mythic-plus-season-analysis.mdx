I pulled every Mythic+ leaderboard the [Battle.net API](https://develop.battle.net/documentation/world-of-warcraft/game-data-apis)
exposes for the current season: each connected realm, each dungeon, each
week. Groups show up on several realms' boards when their players come from
different realms, so I dedup those. What's left:

  <div class="tile"><b>3.11M</b><span>unique runs</span></div>
  <div class="tile"><b>175</b><span>connected realms, EU + US</span></div>

One caveat before the charts. A leaderboard keeps the top 500 runs per realm,
dungeon and week. Keys that got abandoned are not in here. Keys that finished
over the timer are. 87% of what's below was timed.

## Where people play

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/runs-by-level" alt="Bar chart of run count by keystone level, 2 to 23. Levels 10 and 12 stand at about 600 thousand runs each; 11 is half that." caption="Runs by key level. +10 and +12 alone are 39% of the season." />

+10 and +12 are where the rewards sit, so that's where the runs are. Nobody
runs an 11 on purpose.

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/timed-rate-by-level" alt="Line chart of the share of runs completed inside the timer by key level. About 96% up to +6, mid 80s from +11 up." caption="Share of leaderboard runs inside the timer. Past +10 the boards have room for depleted keys too." />

## Nobody's median misses the timer

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/clear-time-by-dungeon" alt="Eight small line charts, one per dungeon, of median clear time in minutes against key level with a dashed line at the dungeon's timer. Every median stays under its timer at all levels." caption="Median clear time per dungeon, dashed line is the timer. Even at +21 the median run is under it." />

I expected the median to cross the timer around +18. It never does, in any
dungeon. The people who reach +21 are the people who can time +21. At +12
the median run uses 80% of the timer in Murder Row and 86% in The Blinding
Vale, and every other dungeon sits in between.

## Who gets brought

<SpecBars>Share of runs that include each spec. Damage is per slot since every group has three.</SpecBars>

Tank is a three-way argument, healer a two-way one, damage is wide open.
That's the average over all levels. Now split it by level:

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/top-spec-share-by-level" alt="Three line charts on a dark panel, one per role, of the top three specs' share by key level in their class colours. Blood Death Knight climbs from about a quarter of groups at +5 to half at +18; Holy Paladin does the same; Arcane Mage rises from 12% of damage slots to 19%." caption="Top three specs per role by key level, in class colours, icon at the line's end." />

Blood Death Knight is in 26% of groups at +5 and 49% at +18. Holy Paladin,
23% to 49%. The meta people argue about is a +18 meta. At +10 you can bring
what you have.

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/top10-comp-share-by-level" alt="Line chart of the share of runs using one of the ten most common full compositions at each key level. Under 2% through +12, 11% at +18, 37% at +21." caption="Share of runs on one of the ten most common full comps at that level." />

Same thing at the whole-group level, only steeper. Below +13 the ten most
common comps cover under 2% of runs. At +21 they cover 37%.

## The timer gets easier every week

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/week-over-week" alt="Bar chart of median +12 clear time as a percent of the timer by week, labelled with each week's two rotating affixes. Falls from 91% in week one to 76% in week seven, every week lower than the last." caption="Median +12 clear time as a share of the timer, by week, with that week's rotating affixes." />

At a fixed +12, the median went from 91% of the timer in week one to 76% in
week seven, down every single week. Affix rotation doesn't even dent the
line. Gear and routes do.

## Odd ones

<Fig src="wow-mythic-plus/realm-count" alt="Bar chart of median +12 clear time as a percent of the timer against the number of distinct realms in the group, 1 to 5. One realm is slowest at 86%; three or more realms sit around 83%." caption="Median +12 clear time by how many realms the five players come from." />

- Groups from a single realm, which are mostly guild and friends, are the
  slowest. Pugs built through the rating filter beat them by three points.
- 85% of runs are cross-faction. Horde-only groups are 4.6% of runs and the
  slowest split.
- EU is a touch faster than US at +12: 82% of timer against 84%.
- Highest key: +23, four runs. Fastest run against its timer: a +2 Ruby
  Life Pools in 9:08.
